Privacy policy

Last updated 3 October 2026.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Toposcope doesn't ask for your name or email, shows no ads, and doesn't track you across apps or websites.

Your photos stay on your iPhone. You choose each photo with the system photo picker; Toposcope only sees the photos you pick. The photo, its location and the labels are processed on your device and stored only on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Location. When you use your location (Panorama, Live, the map), it is used on your device to work out which peaks are around you. It isn't sent to us or stored by us.

Map and terrain downloads. To draw the terrain, Toposcope downloads public elevation tiles from the AWS Open Data "Terrain Tiles" service and peak data files from GitHub (github.com/Levan2002/toposcope). Like any map, these requests name the map area being downloaded, and those services may log the request (with your IP address) under their own privacy policies. They receive no personal data from us. Maps and place search are provided by Apple Maps.

Purchases. Purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to confirm which purchases belong to your device. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history, used only to provide the features you bought (not linked to your identity, not used for tracking). See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Children. Toposcope doesn't knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children.
